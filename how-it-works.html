How it works

From your front door to your wall

  1. Choose a frame

    Pick one of the six and pay. That frame is now yours and comes off the wall.

  2. Send your photo

    Take one photo of the front of your house using the guide below and send it by email or WhatsApp.

  3. Mallory draws

    She draws your house by hand. Allow two weeks from the day your photo arrives.

  4. It arrives framed

    Wrapped in tissue, ready to hang or to give. Notelets come in the same parcel.

What you get

One drawing, one frame, and cards to send

The portrait£50

An original 7 x 5 pen and ink drawing of your house, in the frame you choose from the current six.

The notelets+ £15

Six cards printed with your drawing on good stock, with envelopes and a sticker Mallory designed.

PostageExtra
