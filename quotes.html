Add a typed line, or your own words

Choose one of these 25 lines, or send your own words: a family name, a date, a house name, a private joke. Your own line can be up to 60 characters, counting spaces. Mallory types it on a real typewriter, cuts it out by hand and pastes it beneath the drawing. It costs nothing extra, and you can leave the drawing plain.
