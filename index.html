The photo guide

How to photograph your house

One good photo is all Mallory needs. Stand across the road, hold your phone sideways, and get the whole front in.

Before you take it

Choose a bright, overcast day or a time when the sun is behind you. Move the bins and the car if you can. Open curtains look better than closed ones.

Tell us what matters

If there is something that makes it yours, such as the wisteria, the blue door or the cat in the window, tell Mallory in the box on your order and she will make sure it is in. You can also ask her to leave something out.

Typed on a real typewriter

Add a typed line, or your own words

Choose one of these 25 lines, or send your own words: a family name, a date, a house name, a private joke. Your own line can be up to 60 characters, counting spaces. Mallory types it on a real typewriter, cuts it out by hand and pastes it beneath the drawing. It costs nothing extra, and you can leave the drawing plain.

    Drawn by Mallory
